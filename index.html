01

Aim

Understand and explore the LCG through an interactive experiment.

PROBLEM IDENTIFIED

Manual generation of LCG sequences requires repeated substitution, modular arithmetic, and checking for repeated values. This can be time-consuming and may lead to calculation errors.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

This virtual laboratory allows users to enter LCG parameters and automatically performs the calculations, displays the substitution steps, generates the sequence, determines the period, and visualizes the generated values.

How the Virtual Lab Works

Input→ Formula→ Step-by-step calculation→ Sequence & period→ Visualization

LINEAR CONGRUENTIAL GENERATOR
Xn+1 = (a × Xn + c) mod m

Start with the seed X₀, then repeatedly apply the recurrence to get the next state.
